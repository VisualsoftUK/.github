# Welcome to Visualsoft and VS Labs

## About Us

VS Labs is the product and innovation arm of Visualsoft, the UK’s unified retail agency.

We develop Shopify apps that extend the platform, connecting POS and eCommerce data, improving digital and physical experiences, and automating time-consuming processes.

We also build in-store tech that bridges the gap between the shop floor and storefront, including Shopify POS implementations and integrated hardware.
